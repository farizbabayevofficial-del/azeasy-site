It started with my brother.

My younger brother wanted to learn Azerbaijani, but books were not working for him. He asked me to create an app for him.

At first, I built AzEasier just for him. Then I decided to try it myself: I did not speak Azerbaijani well either, and I wanted to learn.

I was born in Azerbaijan and spent the first seven years of my life there. That connection stayed with me, even as life took me to another country.

What began as a way to help my brother became a way for me to learn too. Today, AzEasier is available to people around the world who want to learn Azerbaijani.
