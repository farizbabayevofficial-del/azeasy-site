AzEasier press kit

Verified facts and free-to-use images for anyone writing about AzEasier, an app to learn Azerbaijani.

Key facts

  • Publisher: Finalingua.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Course: a quick path of 72 lessons (about 9 hours) and a full course of 1,300+ lessons from A1 to C1.
  • Languages of explanation: Russian, English and French.
  • Price: A1 is free; A2 to C1 are in Premium.
  • Limits: the audio is synthetic and the Azerbaijani course has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.

Short description

AzEasier by Finalingua: learn Azerbaijani with the quick path (72 lessons, about 9 h) or the full course (1,300+ lessons, A1 to C1). Explanations in Russian, English and French. A1 is free. App for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
