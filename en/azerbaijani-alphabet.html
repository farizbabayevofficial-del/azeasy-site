Common traps

  • c sounds like English j, not ts.
  • ı (no dot) and i (dotted) are different vowels.
  • q is a back g, x is a rasping h.
  • ə, ö, ü have no plain English equivalent.

Stress usually falls on the last syllable: ca-VAB.
